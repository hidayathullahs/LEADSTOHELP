# 01 — Project Brief: LEADSTOHELP AI

## 1. Project Overview
* **Project Name:** LEADSTOHELP AI
* **Tagline:** *From supply-chain signals to verified business action.*
* **Product Type:** AI-Native Retail Operations Control Tower & Autonomous Procurement Platform.
* **Target Audience:** Independent café owners, bakeries, cloud kitchens, convenience stores, and neighborhood retail managers in India / APAC.
* **Seed Store Reference:** *Deccan Roast Specialty Coffee & Bakery* (Bengaluru, Karnataka).

---

## 2. Core Value Proposition
Moving small business operators from fragmented spreadsheets and reactive emergency runs to proactive, deterministic closed-loop supply chain intelligence with verified financial execution:

$$\text{DETECT} \longrightarrow \text{INVESTIGATE} \longrightarrow \text{PREDICT} \longrightarrow \text{SIMULATE} \longrightarrow \text{RECOMMEND} \longrightarrow \text{NEGOTIATE} \longrightarrow \text{HUMAN APPROVAL} \longrightarrow \text{EXECUTE} \longrightarrow \text{VERIFY} \longrightarrow \text{LEARN}$$

---

## 3. Scope Categorization (MoSCoW Matrix)

### Must Have (P0)
* Continuous stockout run-rate forecasting based on historical sales velocity and lead times.
* 7-Factor Supply Risk Radar ($0\text{--}100$) with explainable dimensions.
* Continuous measured Supplier Reliability Intelligence based on actual SLA performance.
* Multi-Scenario Procurement Simulator comparing Single Supplier vs. Split Order vs. Delay Purchase.
* Multimodal Gemini 2.5 Flash Vision invoice extraction with 3-way PO matching and 8-factor discrepancy detection.
* Human-in-the-Loop Approval Queue with immutable audit logging.
* Production Cloud Run readiness with zero secrets in containers and strict RBAC.

### Should Have (P1)
* Contextual "Ask AI" Copilot drawer for cross-system operational inquiries.
* Autonomous resilience supplier failure recovery flow.
* Developer and manager Agent Run Inspector with live telemetry trace.

### Could Have (P2)
* Automated WhatsApp / SMS vendor notification dispatch.
* Multi-store regional inventory rebalancing.

### Won't Have (Out of Scope for Initial Release)
* Fully autonomous payment execution without human manager sign-off.
* Direct physical IoT hardware integrations (smart scale weighing).
